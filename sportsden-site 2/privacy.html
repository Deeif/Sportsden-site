Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version

SportsDen doesn't have accounts and doesn't collect personal information from people who use the dashboard.

The SportsDen dashboard

  • No sign-up, no tracking. We don't ask for your name, email or any account details.
  • Settings stay on your device. Your board layout and refresh settings are saved in your own browser's local storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Live data. To show scores and win probabilities, your browser requests public data directly from third-party sports and data providers. Those providers receive standard technical information (such as your IP address) as part of any web request, under their own privacy policies.

SportsDen Studio and TikTok

SportsDen Studio is an internal tool used only by the SportsDen team. With our own TikTok account's permission, it uploads videos we made to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts, which we then review and post (or delete) ourselves.

  • It uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API with the user.info.basic and video.upload permissions, for our own account only.
  • It stores only the sign-in tokens TikTok issues, kept encrypted, to send drafts on our behalf.
  • It does not access, collect or store information about other TikTok users, and does not post anything publicly on its own.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).

Sharing

We don't sell or share personal information, because we don't collect it.

Children

SportsDen is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.
